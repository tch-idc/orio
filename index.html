Happy Birthday, Orin. ♡

Honestly, kotha gula kotha theke shuru korbo shetao bujhtesi na. August 2024 er shei shomoy theke aj porjonto koto kichu hoye gelo, right?

Back in August 2024, I had no idea je ekdin tumi amar life-er eto important ekta part hoye jaba. Then came September 16, 2024. The day you said yes. Maybe it looked like just another date back then, but for me, it became the beginning of something I'll always remember.

We've had our fair share of arguments, fights, misunderstandings, and difficult situations. Shob shomoy shobkichu perfect chilo na. Sometimes things got really difficult, and there were moments when we probably didn't know how to handle everything. But every chapter became a part of our story.

And then came those months when I was sick and had to disappear offline. 5–6 months. I know I couldn't give you the time, attention, or presence you deserved during that period. There was so much I couldn't do, so much I couldn't say, and so many moments I couldn't be a part of. But I'm grateful that you knew what was happening and that we eventually found our way back to talking again.

I don't want to pretend that our journey has been perfect. It hasn't. But I wouldn't erase the memories that made it ours.

And today, it's your birthday. Your third birthday since we became us.

I hope this new year of your life brings you the happiness you've always deserved. I hope you get to experience beautiful things, achieve everything you've been working towards, and find countless reasons to smile. I hope life becomes a little kinder to you, and that even on your hardest days, you never forget how valuable you are.

I might not always have the perfect words, and I know I'm not perfect either. But I genuinely wish the best for you, not just today, but every single day.

Thank you for being a part of my story, Orin. Thank you for all the memories, the conversations, the laughter, and even the little moments that might seem insignificant but mean something to me.

Happy 9th November, my Orin.

Here's to you, to your happiness, and to all the beautiful chapters life still has waiting for you.

With love,
Jarif ♡
